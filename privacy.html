Privacy Policy

Effective 1 August 2026

This policy explains what allinone (“the Service”, operated by ALLINONE INC.) collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted. allinone is a social media scheduling tool: you connect your own social media accounts, and it publishes posts to them on your behalf.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address and a one-way hash of your password. If you sign in with Google instead, we store the name, email address and profile picture Google provides, along with Google’s stable identifier for your account.

Connected social accounts. When you connect a social platform we store the access and refresh tokens that platform issues, the scopes you granted, and basic public profile details for the connected account — its identifier, username or handle, display name and profile picture — so the connection can be shown to you and used to publish.

Content you create. The captions, titles, media links, schedules and per-platform settings of the posts you compose, plus the publishing outcome for each platform (success, failure and any error the platform returned).

Performance data. Aggregate engagement figures for posts published through the Service — impressions, likes, comments, shares and clicks.

We do not collect your social media password. Authentication happens on the platform’s own site and we only ever receive a token.

2. TikTok data specifically

If you connect a TikTok account, we request the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish, and we receive and store:

  • your TikTok open_id, username, display name and avatar URL — used to label the connection in the interface;
  • an access token and refresh token — used solely to publish the videos you schedule, and refreshed automatically so scheduled posts still work later;
  • your creator settings as reported by TikTok — which audiences you may post to, and whether comments, Duet or Stitch are disabled on your account — used to show you only the options TikTok will accept;
  • the video files and captions you choose to publish, which are passed to TikTok for posting.

TikTok data is used only to provide the publishing features you ask for. It is never used for advertising, profiling or training machine learning models, and it is never sold or licensed to anyone. Disconnecting a TikTok account in the Service deletes its tokens and profile details from our records; you can also revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately stops the Service being able to post.

3. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To publish and schedule the posts you create, to the accounts you connected.
  • To check the health of your connections and warn you before a post fails.
  • To show you performance data for what you published.
  • To notify you when a post fails or a connection needs reconnecting.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing with third parties

We share data only where it is necessary to deliver a feature you used:

  • The social platforms you connect (TikTok, YouTube/Google, Facebook/Meta, LinkedIn and others). Post content, media and settings are sent to the platform you selected in order to publish. Their own privacy policies then apply to that content.
  • Anthropic, if you use the optional AI caption assistant. Only the topic you type and the target platform are sent — never your tokens, your connected accounts, or your other posts.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to. We do not otherwise share, rent or sell data to third parties.

5. Storage, security and retention

Data is stored in the Service’s database and retained for as long as your account exists. Deleting a connected account deletes that connection’s tokens immediately. Deleting a post deletes its content and its publishing records. Deleting your account deletes everything associated with it, including all connected-account tokens.

Access tokens are the most sensitive data we hold and are treated as credentials: they are used only for the publishing operations you requested, and are never exposed to the browser except where a platform’s own upload protocol requires the browser to send the file directly.

6. Your rights

  • Access — you can view your connected accounts, posts and performance data in the app at any time.
  • Deletion — you can disconnect any social account, delete any post, or delete your account entirely.
  • Revocation — you can revoke the Service’s access from within each social platform’s own settings, independently of us.
  • Any other request — including a copy or erasure request under GDPR, the UK GDPR or CCPA — can be sent to gderbew@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the social platforms you connect, whichever is higher), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
